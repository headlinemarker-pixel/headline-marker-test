先行サポーターについて

We call customers who subscribe to a paid plan while the extension is still under development “Early Supporters.”

Early Supporters receive the following benefits:

  • A 🌟 (Early Supporter badge) will appear next to your plan in the extension popup.
  • You can use the paid plan at a discounted Early Supporter price.
  • As long as you keep your paid subscription active, you can continue at the Early Supporter price even after new features are added.
  • Future Pro features will also be available at an Early Supporter Pro price.

※However, if continued operation becomes difficult due to tax changes, platform policy changes, or significant economic conditions, we may revise prices. Also, new sign-ups at the Early Supporter price may end without notice.
※If the Early Supporter price ends and you cancel and re-subscribe later, the regular price at that time will apply.
